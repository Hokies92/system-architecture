    What the saved Space fixes about the Boundary

    What is and is not verified. Two separate checks against web sources covered every view and every geometry. They confirmed each citation's author, title and year, and corrected or re-sourced the rules that did not hold. Most confirmations rest on secondary sources; the primary text was opened for Meadows, Lewin (1936), Shannon (1949), Sorokin and Gauss only. Still unconfirmed: that Bourdieu's second axis and the link between position and taste appear in Distinction itself (confirmed for his 1985 article), and Hutchinson's and von Bertalanffy's rules in their own words. "Look it up" opens a Wikipedia search as a starting point. Each view's starting property values, the rule that picks the geometry, and the Space-to-Boundary links are readings made for this page. Twenty-seven views is broad, not exhaustive.
